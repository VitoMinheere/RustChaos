# Urban Chaos: the story, mission by mission

Everything the game's missions tell, written out: briefings, dialogue, characters, objectives, side jobs, items, and how each mission is won or lost. It covers the 33 released missions and the unreleased ones that are still in the install. It's meant as a reference for writing new stories that fit the existing world and lore.

## How this was made, and how to read it

Everything is taken from the game's own files:
- **Mission files** (`levels\*.ucm`): who appears, what they say and when, items, beacons, wins and losses.
- **`data\urban.sty`**: titles, briefings, unlock order and districts.
- **Chatter files** (`text\*.txt`): what passers-by say.
- **The original source code**: where it explains something.

The files are generated by `tools/story/` from the mission files and hand-written notes; see its README to rebuild them.

The story summaries, the names given to unnamed people, and the titles of the unreleased missions are written by hand from that material. The original never names most people, so a name like "Wildcat (red) #74" is the person's type plus the number of the event point that makes them. The game's three videos (`bink\*.bik`) aren't transcribed here.

This file holds the world, the cast and the story as a whole, then lists the missions with a short story for each. Each mission's full write-up is a file of its own in [`scripts/`](scripts/). It has:
- **Briefing**: as the menu shows it.
- **Story**: what happens, in order.
- **Characters**: who matters.
- **Everyone the script makes** (folded): every person the script creates by name or by role, with crowds summarised.
- **Objects**: items, vehicles, creatures, street furniture, and set pieces like steam vents and lifts.
- **Badges** and **side objectives**: as this engine's checklist shows them.
- **Beacons**: the places and people the radar points to.
- **How it ends**: every way the mission can be won or lost.
- **Script** (folded): every line, roughly in the order it can happen, with who says it and what sets it off.

On the script:
- "Despatch (radio)" is a line with no speaker, shown as coming over the radio. Despatch, the Chief and other voices share it.
- "Street sign" lines name the street you've walked into.
- The order is worked out from what each line waits on, so lines that can happen at any time are mixed in by when they first become possible.

## The factions

**The UCPD.** Union City's police: understaffed, mocked by the press, partly corrupt.
- **Command:** the Chief gives the orders; Despatch talks Darci through her shifts; Sergeant Bryant runs cases from the station.
- **Rot inside:** a mole tips off dealers (Grim Gardens); Sergeant Smyth works with the Wildcats (Semtex); cops say "orders from on high" mean cooperating with them (Arms Breaker); Officer Bellows is suspiciously keen at a crime scene (The Fallen).
- **No help from above:** by the end the National Guard never comes, and Washington sends only Roper.

**The Wildcats, or the Wildcat Brotherhood.** A small-time gang that grew powerful overnight because Bane took it under his wing.
- **Look:** three colours of members: rasta, red and grey.
- **Organisation:** chapters (the Southside chapter is the most organised and the most dangerous), all answering to "the Boss".
- **What they say:** they call outsiders "sinners", "city-scum" and "non-believers", say "there's a CHANGE comin'", and promise the UCPD's reign is over.
- **What they do:** arms caches, car bombs, extortion and kidnappings, then open war on the police: a quota of one cop killed a day, cops taken hostage, a march on UCPD HQ.
- **What they don't know:** most rank-and-file Wildcats only learn late (Stern Revenge) that they have "been working for Mack Bane all along".

**The Fallen.** Bane's cult, and his personal bodyguards.
- **The Men In Black:** men in tailored black suits who "look like Wall Street chumps", carry hand-held miniguns, and explode when killed. They call Darci "foul whore of Union City" and the people "animals". One of them is named **Claavus**.
- **Their human servants:** for example **Braddock**, a Wildcat who executes shop owners.
- **Their creed:** the city is "marked for death", and "burning rain" will fall "as Master Bane foresaw".

**Bane's backers.** The **money boys at Gaemin Research**, who bankroll the missile programme, and "someone big in City Hall" (Gordansky's lead).

**The press.** **UCNN** and its reporter **Gordansky**, who hound the UCPD on air and keep getting kidnapped while chasing the Wildcats. "Art Belly" is heard on late-night radio.

**Mako's side.** The Wildcats as seen from inside, through **Mako**, an out-of-town enforcer brought in by the Boss.

## The cast

**Darci (D'arci) Stern.** The player. A rookie cop on her first day in RTA. Blunt, quick with an insult, and steadily promoted from "Rookie" to "Super Rookie" by the end. Her late father was a cop. Deeks was his snitch, and Roper knew him well enough to say "your father would not be happy if I allowed you to be harmed".

**Roper.** A mysterious older man.
- **First meeting:** he introduces himself in Trouble in the Park as someone who knew Darci's father.
- **His role:** he supplies equipment and turns up "at the weirdest times". To the police he's an agent sent from Washington (FBI? CIA? NSA? "it's classified"). The Wildcats call him "the old man".
- **His past:** Bane says he has known Roper and Darci "for almost a thousand years".
- **In play:** playable in Stern Revenge, Day Of Reckoning, and parts of Cop Killers and Estate of Emergency.

**Deeks.** A tramp and snitch, forty years on Union City's streets, and Darci's father's old informant. He calls her "girlie", helps with Gun Hunt, and goes into hiding after the Wildcats come for him in Semtex.

**Mack Bane.** Mayoral candidate, backer and secret leader of the Wildcats, head of The Fallen. In the end he's revealed as "an ancient warlock" who summons a Balrog, and in his true form he hovers and raises the dead. He quotes "from Caledonia and ancient Nubia..." (and in Media Trouble a stranger murmurs "...from Nubia and ancient Albion...", possibly him in disguise).

**Mako.** A Wildcat enforcer with a reputation ("I thought you'd be bigger"). He kills cops for fun but won't hurt a kidnapped boy, and says "I ain't foolin' around". Playable in Nitro Car, Rat Catch and the bonus mission Assassin.

**Gordansky.** UCNN reporter, a thorn in the UCPD's side, and the source of the City Hall lead. Kidnapped twice (Media Trouble, Headline Hostage), and also met in Grim Gardens. Darci owes her an exclusive.

**Recurring police:**

- **Officer Mills**: stuck on traffic duty.
- **Sergeant Castor** and **Captain Falcow** (Stern Revenge).

**Wildcats with names:**

- **Shard**: leans on the bomb-parts man Finkel.
- **Spinks**: wonders who "our Man" is.

## The story in three acts

1. **The rookie (training to Gun Hunt).** Darci passes her training and starts on the beat in West District. On her first day she meets Deeks. Small crimes (a crash, muggers, a jumper, a dumped gun) already carry hints: a punk screaming "Union City will fall!", street talk about Bane, vans of AK-47s. Meanwhile, in Nitro Car and Rat Catch, Mako works for the Wildcats from the inside.
2. **The Wildcats rise (Trouble in the Park to Southside Offensive).**
   - **Roper appears** and names Mack Banes as the Wildcats' backer.
   - **Darci works up the gang:** a gang leader brought in at the stadium, arms caches, Gordansky kidnapped, a district stormed, car bombs (whose bombers she recognizes from Nitro Car's job), drugs in the Botanical Gardens, Semtex on the docks.
   - **Open war on the police:** a corrupt sergeant exposed, two cops taken hostage, Southside overrun.
   - **The real enemy:** Roper finally says the Wildcats are only the tip of it: an outside, far more potent force controls them.
3. **The cult (Psycho Park to Day Of Reckoning).**
   - **The Fallen surface:** the city burns; the Men In Black's murders lead to "City Hall"; Darci is kidnapped and Roper rescues her.
   - **The case against Bane:** Gordansky reveals Bane leads the Wildcats and plans a subliminal TV broadcast, which Darci stops. Bane flees to his estate, where he speaks of a thousand years. His files point to missiles built with Gaemin Research money, and Darci destroys them in the snow.
   - **The end:** Bane reveals himself as an ancient warlock and summons a Balrog. The last fight is on the roofs of Gunston Tower against Bane himself. The unreleased jungle mission would have followed Bane's men to El Cossa Island.

The released ending (the third video) and the Dreamcast-only secret mission "Breakout!" aren't covered here.

## Threads a new story could pick up

These are hinted at but never settled:
- **Darci's father:** who he was, and how he knew Roper.
- **Who Roper really is:** a Washington agent, yet known to Bane "for almost a thousand years".
- **The "Caledonia and ancient Nubia" line:** what it refers to.
- **The Men In Black:** where they come from, and their miniguns and death-explosions.
- **"The Boss" of the Wildcats:** always unnamed. Is he Bane, or someone between them?
- **The rot in the UCPD:** "orders from on high", Smyth, Bellows, the mole in Bryant's drugs case.
- **City Hall and Gaemin Research:** who else was in it with Bane.
- **Mako:** what happens to him after the Wildcats fall (Assassin is his last mission).
- **Gordansky's exclusive.**
- **El Cossa Island:** the more silos there.
- **Early drafts:** the villain was "Mr X" and then "the Judge" before he was Bane.

## The released missions

In the menu's order (`urban.sty`): each mission unlocks the next, except that the training and street missions interleave and Mako's missions sit among Darci's. Assassin unlocks last, after Day Of Reckoning.

- **[Combat Tutorial](scripts/FTutor1.md)**, Combat Centre: Darci's first day at the Combat Centre. Text instructions walk her through punches and kicks, the grapple, sprinting, the sliding tackle, triple combos, knocking a target down and arresting it, and finally fighting four at once with back and side kicks. The targets are training dummies dressed as Wildcats.
- **[Physical Training](scripts/Assault1.md)**, Assault Course: Officer Dean puts Darci through her physical on the assault course: stick to the track, grab the zip cable, and hit the switch at the end before the two-minute clock runs out. Attacking Dean ends the test.
- **[Driving Bronze](scripts/testdrive1a.md)**, Oval Circuit: Three laps of the oval in a squad car inside 90 seconds, avoiding cones and oil drums. The instructor starts the watch when Darci gets in. Running over the instructor, the other cops or the contractors fixing the station heating ends it.
- **[Combat Silver](scripts/fight1.md)**, Combat Centre: Combat Silver: waves of skilled and sometimes armed sparring partners, each wave against the clock. Clearing the first stage gives the Bronze grade on the way.
- **[RTA](scripts/police1.md)**, West District: Darci's first beat as a rookie in West District. Despatch sends her to an abandoned taxi after a crash on Zarb Avenue, where two cops are dealing with the drunk who caused it; she drives the taxi back to the station's vehicle drop. On the way an attempted homicide comes in from the Clunton Projects: the victim's friend points her to Kleetus Avenue, where three witnesses argue about how many shots they heard. A mugging crew works the area, and she must bring at least one mugger in alive; one waits on a rooftop. An old tramp hails her by name: Deeks, her late father's snitch from his cop days, who promises to help her. Side jobs: arrest the extra muggers, a man urinating in the park, and a lecherous civilian; talk to Deeks.
- **[Driving Silver](scripts/testdrive2.md)**, Advanced Circuit: Two laps of the intermediate course in 120 seconds, with a reverse park into a marked bay on each lap. Hurting or arresting the training officer ends it.
- **[Combat Gold](scripts/fight2.md)**, Combat Centre: Combat Gold: harder waves, tighter clock, and assassins among the sparring partners.
- **[The Jump](scripts/police2.md)**, West District: A jumper is on a ledge of Brooks Tower on Keating Avenue. He's Barnaby, depressed because the Union City Fire Department turned him down, and he jumps if anyone approaches him head-on. Darci climbs the next building and rides the zip line down behind him, then talks him off: he decides he'd rather be a cop. Along the way: officer Miles' squad car has been stolen (a streetwalker saw a 'slick dude' on Lime Street, a Wildcat car thief), and a crazed woman in the park turns out to be 'Crack Suckin' Susan'. A wounded woman lies at a shooting where a punk screamed 'Union City will fall!', the first sign of the Wildcats' war on the city.
- **[Driving Gold](scripts/testdrive3.md)**, Advanced Circuit: The advanced course in a meat wagon: two laps in 140 seconds, with two reverse parks per lap.
- **[Nitro Car](scripts/Bankbomb1.md)**, Gangland: The first mission on the other side of the law: Mako, a Wildcat heavy brought in from outside, is told by the Boss's man to cause CHAOS. He drives a car bomb to the bank courtyard and runs before it blows, then takes a second car bomb (the Boss's explosives expert's work) to a department store guarded by a police bomb squad. Cliffy and Zit, two Wildcats touchy about their nicknames, back him up and take on the cops. Losing either of them, or wrecking a car bomb before it's delivered, fails it.
- **[Gun Hunt](scripts/police3.md)**, West District: An honest citizen saw a man dump a gun behind the Eagle Building, and Deeks knows where. Darci finds him in the park; he points her to the skips in the centre of town. Once she has the weapon, its armed owner comes looking for it, and she must arrest or drop him before taking the gun back to the station. Meanwhile the city is uneasy: a drunk tramp warns of 'dark things on the horizon', people gossip that there's 'something of the night about that Bane character', and a cop says one Wildcat set was caught with a vanload of AK-47s. A disturbance at the diner, where men call the customers SINNERS, gives two more suspects. Killing or arresting Deeks fails it.
- **[Rat Catch](scripts/Gangorder2.md)**, Gangland: Mako again. A Wildcat contact tells him to collect McLean and Bish and get the job from Spiv: the Boss wants the son of McIntyre, a friend of the cops, brought in unharmed. The son bolts. Run him down to half health and he surrenders and follows Mako to the drop. Kill him and the Boss's punishment is five more dead cops, which Mako relishes. The district crawls with police, and losing Spiv, Bish, McLean or the escort ends the job.
- **[Trouble in the Park](scripts/police4.md)**, West District: Gang-bangers are loitering in Bushy Park and the residents, and reporter Gordansky, are complaining. Darci clears the Wildcats out; the punks talk about 'sinners', 'the fallen', and a CHANGE coming, and two of them arrange a meet at the Ballpark 'tonight'. A mystery man who knew her father introduces himself as Roper and tells her what's going on: Mack Banes, the mayoral candidate, has taken the Wildcats under his wing, and three of their leaders meet tonight at the UC Baseball park. Bring one in and he'll talk. Roper must survive.
- **[Gatecrasher](scripts/bball2.md)**, Baseball Ground: A tip-off: three gang leaders meet at the UC 'Black Cats' stadium to carve up their turf after the Bushy Park troubles. Georgie, on the gate, says only Wild Bill can let her in. Bill was the baseball team's manager until the Wildcats pushed him out last month, and he's found drinking at Posheeta's Mexican Diner after a detour through a nightclub full of drunks and dancers. Through the back alley Darci gets into the grounds, where Wildcats hold workmen hostage and talk of Banes and AK-47s, and armed men are pinned in a corner by cops. She has to bring a gang boss in alive. Killing the last one ruins the info for Roper.
- **[Arms Breaker](scripts/wstores1.md)**, Rio canal: The Wildcats keep two arms caches in Darci's sector, and she has to handle it alone before Gordansky and UCNN broadcast the force's failure. Roper calls her to Geebag Street for equipment: the Wildcats 'are more than you think'. She sabotages the stores around Reagan Plaza and the Rio Canal Boathouse and wipes out the pick-up team before their van collects. Overheard: Wildcats have 'plans' for Gordansky via their Southside crew; a Wildcat guard, Spinks, frets that only 'our Man' knows the plan; and two cops say orders from on high mean working with the Wildcats now. Side jobs: haul in 'Bloody' Mary Schwartz from the bar, stop a citizen assault.
- **[Media Trouble](scripts/e3.md)**, Southside Beach: Gordansky snooped too close and the Wildcats have her trussed up in a beach house at the edge of Southside. UCNN will crucify the force if she dies. Roper hands Darci equipment and leaves on 'other matters'. She fights through Southside's Wildcats, who now call outsiders SINNERS and threaten to kill on sight, takes out the lookout and the reporter's guard, and frees her. Gordansky, grateful despite her TV attacks on the UCPD, tells Darci the Wildcats are backed by someone big in City Hall. Along the way: Shard leans on Finkel, who builds the car bomb timers; a man is on the edge of suicide; Mr Shaw is extorted for files; drug dealer Ed Miller runs. Killing the hostage, Roper or the Chief fails it.
- **[Urban Shakedown](scripts/westcrime1.md)**, West District: The gang boss from the stadium squealed under O'Byrne and Kowalski: the Wildcats are massing an army to take West District. The station is attacked and Darci has three minutes to break every wave of Wildcats, Man In Black fighters among them, before the district falls. Officer Schwartz insists on coming along and must be kept alive.
- **[Auto-Destruct](scripts/carbomb1.md)**, Gangland: 'Terrorists' have planted a car bomb outside the bank in Gangland and the gangs have sealed the area so the bomb squad can't get in. Darci fights her way in and drives the sedan back to the disposal team before its 90 seconds run out, then a second one outside Harbours department store. She recognizes the bombers. They're Wildcat specialists brought in from out of town, not terrorists in disguise as the Sarge believes, and she arrests both in the park. Searching their den by the derelict buildings turns up a UCNN video tape, which she links to Gordansky's kidnapping. Hurting the bomb engineers or killing the bombers fails it.
- **[Grim Gardens](scripts/botanicc.md)**, Botanical Gardens: Sergeant Bryant spent two months on a drugs ring until someone inside the force tipped off the dealers, who stashed $3 million of narcotics in the closed Botanical Gardens. Darci gets in (the power, the lifts and the high-pressure jets are on switches), finds explosives, and blows both narcotics stores. Gordansky is there following her own trail: the car bombing wasn't a one-off, the Southside Wildcats are dealing for half a ton of explosives. Other threads: a Wildcat's mother scolding her son ('your daddy was a respectable MUGGER'), the parole-breaker Koots, a dealer selling 'close-to-home-made' product, a gunman who shot a cop outside the hotel. The gardens have plaques: 'Twinned with the town of GUILDFORD'. Arresting or killing Gordansky gets Darci suspended.
- **[Semtex](scripts/semtex.md)**, Southside Beach
- **[Cop Killers](scripts/AWOL1.md)**, Rio canal: Roper's warning comes true: the Wildcats have declared war on the UCPD and snatched officers Johnson and Beck, threatening to shoot them unless the police back off. The streets around the Rio canal are sown with military-surplus mines. Roper turns up ('you ain't the only friend I got in the UCPD'): Darci frees Johnson and walks him to the safe zone, then play passes to Roper, who carries the wounded Beck back. Overheard: the Southside Wildcats have 'all KIND o' artillery' cooking for the UCPD.
- **[Southside Offensive](scripts/mission2.md)**, Southside Beach: The Chief sends every officer to Southside, where crime is soaring and three cops are already down. Darci, partnered with the joking Officer Green, has to drive the crime rate down: dealers, a stalker, a Wildcat (Sweeney) about to settle a score with Fish, an arms handover to Mr Shultz, and Griz executing Walker for talking to Gordansky at Mable Greer's diner. A body turns up with chunks of flesh missing. Roper finally explains himself: the Wildcats aren't the root of it; they're controlled by an outside, far more potent force, and he'll help destroy them. Green and Roper must survive.
- **[Psycho Park](scripts/park2.md)**, Central Park: Neighbourhoods are blowing up all over town, and a large armed force of Wildcats marches on Central Park and UCPD HQ beside it. Roper joins Darci, and together they take down the storm squads, the 'Falcon group', the park murder squad, a lone psycho and the Wildcat kingpin's crew. The kingpin turns out to be a diversion: if any squad reaches HQ's main doors, the station falls. Citizens ask whether a cult is behind the madness and where the National Guard is. (The original plays a video here; its bats are compiled out.)
- **[The Fallen](scripts/MIB.md)**, Southside Beach: A body cut apart by hundreds of bullets lies by Mable Greer's Diner in Southside, and City Hall wants five clues. Officers Harney and Estevez are on the scene; a second body, Albert Haines the bootlegger, turns up, and Pete's wife is looking for her husband. Darci follows the trail: a diner napkin, a Dancing Fool nightclub brochure, a note to 'meet Claavus at the Vote Bane sign' in the park, a 'Claavus eyes only' file, and a 'City Hall' video tape. She witnesses Braddock, a Wildcat in the service of the cult, executing shop owners who stood up to them, and meets the killers themselves: men in tailored black suits with hand-held miniguns, who explode when they die. They call themselves a brotherhood that will bring death on the city; one of them, Claavus, dismisses Darci as 'merely one of the animals'. Officer Bellows turns up suspiciously keen on the crime scene.
- **[Stern Revenge](scripts/skymiss2.md)**, Skyline: Told by Roper ('now it's my turn to pay her back'). The Wildcats are snatching people off the streets to tie up the police, and they've knocked Darci cold and taken her too. Sent by Captain Falcow, Roper (taken by the cops for an FBI or CIA man) brushes past Sergeant Castor, with an academy friend of Darci's tagging along, frees her, and then they free the other hostages. Wildcats talk of working for Mack Bane 'all along', of big plans for the TV station, and of Bane revealing himself to the Brothers. Darci blows up the trucks before they take the hostages away. (The original's bats are compiled out; the mission asks for them.)
- **[Transmission Terminated](scripts/factory1.md)**, Television Centre: Gordansky has revealed what she knew: Mack Bane, candidate in the UC 2000 mayoral race, is the Wildcats' covert leader, and he has subliminal technology that interferes with digital encoding, to be used in a TV address tonight. A hostage situation at the UC Metal Works pulls the police away from the UCNN TV station, and Darci goes as backup. She gets over the perimeter, frees the workers, and secretly finds explosives to blow the TV transmitter before Bane broadcasts. Also: a sniper on a roof, dealers in the parking lot, citizens who heard 'Art Belly' on the radio, and officer Beck, back on duty.
- **[Estate of Emergency](scripts/estate2.md)**, Banes Estate: Bane is retreating to his out-of-town estate before fleeing abroad, and the UCPD needs him alive. Darci gets past the guards, Roper (now openly sent by Washington at the Chief's request) saves her life, and she tells him she overheard Wildcats talking about MISSILES aimed at Union City. Inside, Bane speaks to her: he knows her and 'the old man, Roper' and has known them both 'for almost a thousand years'. She has to take the security system offline from the pool house and arrest Bane before his getaway helicopter is refuelled. Killing him, or letting him fly, fails it.
- **[Seek, Sneak and Seize](scripts/Stealtst1.md)**, Medical Centre: Roper searched Bane's estate, pulled the drives and found an encrypted file, 'evidence that Bane is in league with higher forces', but has only half the key. The other half is in the Gaemin Research Center. Darci sneaks in (firing a gun raises the alarm), steals the confidential file, and meets Roper at the front gates in his van. Two guards argue over whether Bane really means 'Armageddon' and the missiles.
- **[Target UC](scripts/snow2.md)**, Snow Plains: The decrypted files show Bane and his money men at Gaemin Research have been building missiles in secret. Three launch sites in the Snow Plains, guarded by Wildcats and Bane's personal bodyguards, 'the Fallen' (they look like Wall Street chumps). Darci takes explosives from the village and destroys all three rockets before the warheads are primed. Workmen start the launch sequence if they see her.
