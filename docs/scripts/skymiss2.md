# Stern Revenge

*A released mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\skymiss2.ucm`, map `skymap30.iam`, district Skyline

**Played as:** Roper

**Briefing:**

> The Wildcats have gone ahead with their plan of capturing hostages to clog up all the Police resources in Union City. And somehow, they got to Stern - knocked her cold and took her hostage. Now it's my turn to pay her back - in full. I'll get her out and kick every Wildcat ass there is on the way!

**Story:** Told by Roper ('now it's my turn to pay her back'). The Wildcats are snatching people off the streets to tie up the police, and they've knocked Darci cold and taken her too. Sent by Captain Falcow, Roper (taken by the cops for an FBI or CIA man) brushes past Sergeant Castor, with an academy friend of Darci's tagging along, frees her, and then they free the other hostages. Wildcats talk of working for Mack Bane 'all along', of big plans for the TV station, and of Bane revealing himself to the Brothers. Darci blows up the trucks before they take the hostages away. (The original's bats are compiled out; the mission asks for them.)

- **Captain Falcow** (mentioned): sent Roper
