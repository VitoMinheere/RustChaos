# Transmission Terminated

*A released mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\factory1.ucm`, map `tv1.iam`, district Television Centre

**Played as:** Darci

**Briefing:**

> Gordansky's spilled the beans on what she had that was so upsetting to the Wild Cats. It turns out that Mack Bane, candidate for the UC 2000 Mayoral race, is connected high on his perch to the Wild Cats - Gordansky even claims he's the mysterious gang's covert leader. Bane is said to have ultra-secret subliminal technology that interferes with digital encoding. Gordansky says that a TV address to the city tonight will be "contaminated" with this technology, and it MUST be secretly destroyed. Not only that, but as a distraction for the cops, they took over the UC Metalwork's factory, and multiple hostages have been taken! We've got 30 minutes to get to the studio and shut it down - permanently.

**Story:** Gordansky has revealed what she knew: Mack Bane, candidate in the UC 2000 mayoral race, is the Wildcats' covert leader, and he has subliminal technology that interferes with digital encoding, to be used in a TV address tonight. A hostage situation at the UC Metal Works pulls the police away from the UCNN TV station, and Darci goes as backup. She gets over the perimeter, frees the workers, and secretly finds explosives to blow the TV transmitter before Bane broadcasts. Also: a sniper on a roof, dealers in the parking lot, citizens who heard 'Art Belly' on the radio, and officer Beck, back on duty.
