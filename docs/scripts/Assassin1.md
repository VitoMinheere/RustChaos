# TV Station Rooftops *(unofficial title; `Assassin1`)*

*An unreleased mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\Assassin1.ucm`, map `submiss1a.iam`

**Played as:** Darci

**Story:** Another early take on Transmitter, on the old-layout map `submiss1a.iam`: work over the rooftops to the downtown district and destroy the TV station's transmitting satellite dish, through a sector 'patrolled heavily by opposition gangs'. No ending is scripted. Despite the file's name it has nothing to do with the released Assassin (`Gangorder1`).
