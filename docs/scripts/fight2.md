# Combat Gold

*A released mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\fight2.ucm`, map `fight1.iam`, district Combat Centre

**Played as:** Darci

**Briefing:**

> Use your skills to eliminate OR arrest ALL opponent targets. Gold award pits you against waves of increasingly difficult opponents. You must defeat each wave within the time limits.

**Story:** Combat Gold: harder waves, tighter clock, and assassins among the sparring partners.

**Characters:**

- **Darci Stern**
- Sparring partners in Wildcat colours: guards, Man In Black fighters and assassins
