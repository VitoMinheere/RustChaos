*An unreleased mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\Crimescene.ucm`, map `southside.iam`

**Played as:** Darci

**Story:** An unfinished Southside investigation: a man shot with two .44 slugs, 'not no gang-banger'. A witness, beaten to half health, describes two killers: one with a handgun, the other dressed in black and HUGE. A silencer goes to forensics; the trail leads to Harley Blvd. An early sketch of The Fallen and its Men In Black.
