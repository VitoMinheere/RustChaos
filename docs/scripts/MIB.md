# The Fallen

*A released mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\MIB.ucm`, map `southside.iam`, district Southside Beach

**Played as:** Darci

**Briefing:**

> A freshly cut up body down in Southside UC. Nobody saw a thing, and I got the feeling we ain't gonna learn SHIT from the locals. City Hall has been on our asses about our lousymurder prosecution rate, so we need you to turn up at least FIVE clues from this murder trail. But go easy on this one D'arci - We're getting bad reports about the Southside street crime and the local Wildcat chapter seems much more organized now!

**Story:** A body cut apart by hundreds of bullets lies by Mable Greer's Diner in Southside, and City Hall wants five clues. Officers Harney and Estevez are on the scene; a second body, Albert Haines the bootlegger, turns up, and Pete's wife is looking for her husband. Darci follows the trail: a diner napkin, a Dancing Fool nightclub brochure, a note to 'meet Claavus at the Vote Bane sign' in the park, a 'Claavus eyes only' file, and a 'City Hall' video tape. She witnesses Braddock, a Wildcat in the service of the cult, executing shop owners who stood up to them, and meets the killers themselves: men in tailored black suits with hand-held miniguns, who explode when they die. They call themselves a brotherhood that will bring death on the city; one of them, Claavus, dismisses Darci as 'merely one of the animals'. Officer Bellows turns up suspiciously keen on the crime scene.

**Characters:**

- **Darci Stern**
- **Officers Harney** (rookie) and **Sgt. Estevez**

- **Officer Bellows**: takes over the scene, oddly
- **Tallon**: executed as a non-believer
- A tenant hearing men on his roof, a new beach-house owner who hates the UCPD
