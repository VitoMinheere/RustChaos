# Media Trouble

*A released mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\e3.ucm`, map `snocrime.iam`, district Southside Beach

**Played as:** Darci

**Briefing:**

> Stern, that dumb-ass reporter Gordansky snooped a bit too close to the Wild Cats, and got herself collared for her trouble. This is urgent, we need you on the scene ASAP - get Gordansky back to the station, and teach those damn Wild Cats a lesson while you're at it. I don't think I need to tell you what a crucifying we'd get from UCNN if we fail on this one..!

**Story:** Gordansky snooped too close and the Wildcats have her trussed up in a beach house at the edge of Southside. UCNN will crucify the force if she dies. Roper hands Darci equipment and leaves on 'other matters'. She fights through Southside's Wildcats, who now call outsiders SINNERS and threaten to kill on sight, takes out the lookout and the reporter's guard, and frees her. Gordansky, grateful despite her TV attacks on the UCPD, tells Darci the Wildcats are backed by someone big in City Hall. Along the way: Shard leans on Finkel, who builds the car bomb timers; a man is on the edge of suicide; Mr Shaw is extorted for files; drug dealer Ed Miller runs. Killing the hostage, Roper or the Chief fails it.

**Characters:**

- **Darci Stern**
- **Gordansky**: UCNN reporter held hostage
- **Roper**
- **The Chief**: on the scene; mustn't be hurt
- **Finkel**: bomb-maker's supplier, short of explosive
- **Shard**: Wildcat leaning on Finkel
- **Mr Shaw**: extorted for files
- **Ed Miller**: drug dealer (side job)
- A man ready to die, a mugging victim, a cop with a car key, Wildcat lookouts
