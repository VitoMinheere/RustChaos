# Bank Sector Takeover *(unofficial title; `Takeover1`)*

*An unreleased mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\Takeover1.ucm`, map `jumper1.iam`

**Played as:** Darci

**Story:** An early prototype from before Bane: 'Mr X's gang have overrun the banking sector and sealed off the surrounding area.' Darci gets in with a keycard and rescues two workers, taking them out of the front gates to the extraction point, while cops wait for backup. A civilian introduces himself as 'Mark, I'm a programmer'.

**Characters:**

- **Darci Stern**
- **Mr X** (mentioned): the early villain
- Two hostage workers, nervous cops
