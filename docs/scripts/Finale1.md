*A released mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\Finale1.ucm`, map `Rooftest2.iam`, district Clandon Heights

**Played as:** Roper, then Darci

**Briefing:**

> D'arci, this is our final reckoning. The Wildcats are virtually wiped out, the remnants of their leadership dying or dead. But before we walk away from this, the worst is yet to come.

**Story:** Gunston Tower, Clandon Heights. The Wildcats are almost gone and Bane has fled to the rooftops with his last men and his hostages to play out the end of his prophecy. One police team has already been torn apart up there. Roper goes first: he must carry three badly wounded civilians to safety. Then Darci knocks out the two fuel pumps and destroys the fuel reservoir, past steam vents, moving platforms, a gargoyle and Bane himself, who hovers, raises the dead around him, and can't be hurt. (The original plays a video at the end.)
