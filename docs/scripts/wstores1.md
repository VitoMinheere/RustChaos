# Arms Breaker

*A released mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\wstores1.ucm`, map `gpost3.iam`, district Rio canal

**Played as:** Darci

**Briefing:**

> Stern, we may have got a break: We got confirmed reports that the 'Cats have two arms caches somewhere in your sector - that means you have to handle this on your own! Find out where the hell those weapons are kept before that pain in the ass Gordansky and UCNN broadcast our screw-up all over the state!

**Story:** The Wildcats keep two arms caches in Darci's sector, and she has to handle it alone before Gordansky and UCNN broadcast the force's failure. Roper calls her to Geebag Street for equipment: the Wildcats 'are more than you think'. She sabotages the stores around Reagan Plaza and the Rio Canal Boathouse and wipes out the pick-up team before their van collects. Overheard: Wildcats have 'plans' for Gordansky via their Southside crew; a Wildcat guard, Spinks, frets that only 'our Man' knows the plan; and two cops say orders from on high mean working with the Wildcats now. Side jobs: haul in 'Bloody' Mary Schwartz from the bar, stop a citizen assault.
