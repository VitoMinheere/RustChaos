# The Credits Level *(unofficial title; `creds1`)*

*An unreleased mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\creds1.ucm`, map `creds1.iam`

**Played as:** Darci

**Story:** The credits as a walkable level: 'So this is Mucky Foot!' Areas announce PROGRAMMING, DESIGN, ART and SPECIAL THANKS, and the people standing about, as civilians and Wildcats, are the development team, each saying their own name when spoken to (Mike Diskett adds 'Smell my shorts'). Five 'Work Experience?' civilians round it off.

**Characters:**

- **Darci Stern**
- The Mucky Foot team, as themselves
