# Psycho Park

*A released mission of Urban Chaos. Back to [the story overview](../STORY.md).*

`levels\park2.ucm`, map `parkypsx03.iam`, district Central Park

**Played as:** Darci

**Briefing:**

> We got a serious problem down at Union park. Looks like we pushed those Wildcats scum too far. We got a report just in that a large contingent of armed Wildcats has been seen approaching the park. Get the hell down there and do your utmost to secure the area! And show NO MERCY to those punks!

**Story:** Neighbourhoods are blowing up all over town, and a large armed force of Wildcats marches on Central Park and UCPD HQ beside it. Roper joins Darci, and together they take down the storm squads, the 'Falcon group', the park murder squad, a lone psycho and the Wildcat kingpin's crew. The kingpin turns out to be a diversion: if any squad reaches HQ's main doors, the station falls. Citizens ask whether a cult is behind the madness and where the National Guard is. (The original plays a video here; its bats are compiled out.)

**Characters:**

- **Darci Stern**
- **Roper**
- **The Wildcat kingpin** and his crew
- Storm squads, the Falcon group, the murder squad
- Cops defending HQ; citizens filming for UCNN
