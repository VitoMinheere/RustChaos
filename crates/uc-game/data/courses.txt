# The time trials' courses (#519). Not in the original.
#
# A course starts with its line:
#   course <id> <mission> <laps> <name...>
# The mission is the one whose map the course is on: it's loaded for the map, and its script
# isn't run. Then, in squares (a map square is 256 world units):
#   start <x> <z> <heading>   where the car starts, facing <heading> degrees (0 is down the
#                             map's z, 90 down its x)
#   radius <r>                how near a checkpoint the car has to come
#   traffic <n>               the cars on the roads with traffic on
#   medals <gold> <silver> <bronze>   times for the whole race, in seconds (set from the
#                             tests' bot: gold a little under its time in the police car,
#                             bronze a little over the van's; on the Advanced Circuit, the
#                             oval's share of the original's time limit)
#   cp <x> <z> [<r>]          the checkpoints, in order; the first is the start and finish.
#                             A third number is that checkpoint's own radius
